# Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze Emergency Room patient admissions from **April 2023 to October 2024**.

The dashboard provides insights into patient admissions, waiting time, department referrals, patient demographics, and satisfaction. It was developed to help understand patient trends and support data-driven hospital service planning.

---

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Power BI Data Modeling
* Data Visualization

---

## Data Preparation & Cleaning Workflow

All data preparation and cleaning were performed directly in **Power BI using Power Query**.

The workflow included:

* Reviewing the structure and quality of the dataset
* Cleaning and transforming the data
* Checking and handling data inconsistencies
* Preparing fields for analysis
* Creating appropriate fields and categories for visualization
* Validating the prepared data before building the dashboard

The cleaned data was then used for Power BI data modeling and visualization.

---

## Power BI – Data Modeling & DAX

The prepared dataset was organized in Power BI to support the dashboard analysis.

DAX measures and calculations were used to analyze key performance indicators, including:

* Total Patient Admissions
* Average Waiting Time
* Average Patient Satisfaction
* Patient Admissions by Date and Time
* Department Referrals
* Patient Demographics

The dashboard also includes interactive filtering to explore patient trends across different categories and time periods.

---

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard focuses on the following business questions:

* When is the Emergency Room busiest?
* Which department receives the most patient referrals?
* Which months have the highest number of patient admissions?
* How do waiting time and patient satisfaction relate to hospital services?

---

## Key Findings & Insights

### Peak ER Hours

The Emergency Room is busiest between **10:00 AM and 12:00 PM** and again from **2:00 PM to 5:00 PM**.

Fridays and Saturdays around **11:00 AM** show particularly high patient volumes.

### Department Referrals

**General Practice** received the highest number of patient referrals compared with the other departments analyzed, including Orthopedics, Physiotherapy, Cardiology, and Neurology.

### Monthly Patient Admissions

Patient admissions varied across months. **August to October** had the highest number of admissions, while **January** had the lowest.

### Waiting Time & Satisfaction

The dashboard shows an **average waiting time of 35 minutes** and an **average patient satisfaction score of 5/10**.

---

## Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1. **Add More Staff During Busy Hours**
   Consider assigning additional doctors, nurses, and support staff during peak hours, particularly from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

2. **Improve Patient Service Flow**
   A fast-track process for patients with minor health concerns could help reduce waiting times.

3. **Prepare for Busy Months**
   The hospital can prepare additional staff, beds, medicines, and medical supplies during the higher-admission period from August to October.

4. **Improve Patient Satisfaction**
   Reducing waiting times and keeping patients informed about delays may improve the overall patient experience.

---

## Project Summary

The dashboard analyzed **9,216 Emergency Room patient admissions** from April 2023 to October 2024. The analysis identified peak ER hours, department referral patterns, monthly admission trends, waiting time, and patient satisfaction.

**Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Data Modeling → DAX → Dashboard Development → Insights & Recommendations**

This project demonstrates the use of **Microsoft Power BI for an end-to-end data analytics workflow**, from preparing raw data to creating an interactive dashboard and generating actionable insights.
